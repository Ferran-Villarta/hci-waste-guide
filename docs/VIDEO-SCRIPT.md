# Video script (draft): What Goes Where

Final prototype video, **5–8 minutes**. The brief asks for: a brief overview and context, the design considerations, a walkthrough of the full system, and a reflection based on the heuristic evaluation. **Every member must take part**, so there is one part per member below (about 1 minute each, ~7 minutes in total).

The TA uses this video as a guide to the live demo, so the walkthrough parts follow the same order as `MARKER-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

**How to record:**
- Screen-record the live site on a phone (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record), or on a computer in Chrome's phone view (F12, then Ctrl+Shift+M).
- Each member records their voice over their part. Showing your face in a small corner is optional.
- Before recording, empty Saved (**Remove everything**) so the demo starts clean.
- Upload to YuJa (https://ucalgary.yuja.com/) or to D2L if under 2 GB, and put the link in the portfolio.

## Part 2: Design considerations (≈ 1:15) · *(member name)*
**Show:** an item answer (Rinsed glass jar), the pizza box choice, and the Saved panel.

**Say (draft), main decisions and why:**
- The answer comes first, big and in the bin's colour, so it's clear at a glance.
- "It depends" items ask one simple question instead of a long text.
- Hazardous items show the warning before anything else.
- Every item you open is saved automatically, so Saved becomes your list of things to sort, with ticks for what's done.
- Plain language and the City's own answers, with a link to the source.
- Accessibility: contrast checked, large tap areas (A–Z letters, remove button), works with screen readers.

## Part 3: Walkthrough: finding an answer (≈ 1:30) · *(member name)*
**Do (instructions A–F):**
1. Type "piz", pick **Pizza box**, choose **Greasy** → GREEN BIN; switch to **Clean** → BLUE BIN.
2. Shortcut: type "rinsed glass jar" + search → straight to the answer.
3. Typo "cardbord" → **Did you mean cardboard?**
4. "bike tube" → no-result screen with four ways out; **Report missing item** → Report sent.
5. "battery" → warning first → **Find a drop-off near me** → map → tap a pin.

**Say:** what each step shows (search help, error recovery, safety first).

## Part 4: Walkthrough: Saved and sorting (≈ 1:00) · *(member name)*
